Olivia Rodrigo's Hits

good 4 u

good 4 u is the third song off Rodrigo's debut album SOUR. It is one of her most energetic and rock heavy songs. She wrote the song about her ex moving on from her at a very quick pace, and talks about how happy he (the ex) is and how successful he became along with Rodrigo sounding very emotional. The song captures the anger of the breakup from her perspective and mentions how better off the ex is while she has lost her mind, and spends her nights crying over him.



get him back

get him back is on Rodrigo's sophmore album GUTS. It is about wanting revenge on her ex, while also missing him and wanting him back. It mixes rock, pop and punk rock. She lists all of his flaws while also the things she really liked about him.



the cure

the cure is on Rodrigo's latest album You Seem Pretty Sad For A Girl So In Love. She wrote it about how love cannot heal emotional wounds. She sings about self-doubt and comparison. And she repeats saying im unraveled which depicts her losing control
